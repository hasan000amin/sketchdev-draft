The Studio

A tiny team with big pencils. No engines of war — just paper, patience, and play.

We believe a game does not need explosions to be unforgettable. Ours begin on paper — one pencil sketch at a time — and grow into worlds you can finish, master, and replay. Small games, drawn slowly, played everywhere.
