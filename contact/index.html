Pertanyaan yang Sering Diajukan (FAQ)

❓ Bagaimana cara melaporkan bug?

Kirim email ke support@sketchdev.my.id dengan judul "Bug Report: [Nama Game]". Sertakan screenshot, versi aplikasi, dan jenis perangkat.

❓ Bagaimana cara meminta pengembalian dana?

Semua pembelian diproses oleh Google Play. Untuk pengembalian dana, hubungi Google Play Support terlebih dahulu sesuai kebijakan mereka.

❓ Apakah game bisa dimainkan offline?

Sebagian besar game kami bisa dimainkan offline. Namun beberapa fitur mungkin memerlukan koneksi internet.

❓ Bagaimana cara menghapus data saya?

Kirim permintaan ke support@sketchdev.my.id dengan subjek "Hapus Data". Kami akan memproses dalam 7 hari kerja.

❓ Apakah kalian menerima kerja sama?

Ya! Kirim proposal ke support@sketchdev.my.id dengan subjek "Kerja Sama".

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ How do I report a bug?

Email support@sketchdev.my.id with the subject "Bug Report: [Game Name]". Include screenshots, app version, and device type.

❓ How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Google Play. For refunds, please contact Google Play Support first according to their policy.

❓ Can the games be played offline?

Most of our games can be played offline. However, some features may require an internet connection.

❓ How do I delete my data?

Send a request to support@sketchdev.my.id with the subject "Data Deletion". We will process it within 7 business days.

❓ Do you accept collaborations?

Yes! Send your proposal to support@sketchdev.my.id with the subject "Collaboration".
